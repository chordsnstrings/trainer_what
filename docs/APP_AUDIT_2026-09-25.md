# Complete application audit — 25 September 2026

The repository contains a substantial working development application, but the complete product described in the requirements is not finished. The largest product gap is that **workout AI still requires trainer approval for every generated response**, whereas routine nutrition plans and swaps have an automatic delivery path. Several specialist administration screens, provider adapters and operating workflows remain unimplemented. Passing builds and screenshots do not remove these gaps.

This audit reviewed the public, trainer, subscriber and Superadmin interfaces, their API/domain/provider implementations, background jobs, tests and release evidence against `SCREEN_AND_REQUIREMENTS.md`, `TECHNICAL_BLUEPRINT.md`, `NUTRITION_INTEGRATION_PLAN.md` and the delivery roadmap. It is based on application code at `e5a1df5eeedfc2bdf6d89530605d8b844176763d`; `cd225701f876f391c8aa4c3a7460fbe6378030b5` subsequently recorded verification evidence. The audit changes documentation only. It does not fix the defects listed below or qualify real providers.

**How to read the status:** “Created” means there is persisted implementation, with the stated engineering evidence. “Partial” identifies a real implementation that does not yet meet the full requirement. “Missing” identifies code or workflow still to build. “Unverified” identifies existing code whose provider, device or operational behavior has not been demonstrated. No percentage is assigned: the 75 source screen groups are requirements, not 75 equally sized tasks, and 94 captured routes do not imply those groups are complete.

## Nutrition: the exact boundary

The agreed experience is implemented in a basic connected form: the combined tier opens nutrition, the coach teaches and qualifies a policy, and routine valid weekly plans and swaps are delivered without individual coach approval. Meals connect to recipe/cooking choices, portions and weekly groceries. Model-selected plans are checked against stored facts and policy constraints. Photos and barcodes create editable drafts that the subscriber confirms before they enter the diary.

“Trainer-taught AI” currently means private versioned examples and rules used as model context. It does not mean a separately fine-tuned model for each trainer.

The remaining gaps affect the substance of personalization:

1. **Calorie targets are one value per goal.** `nutritionTarget()` matches the profile goal and returns that policy target. There is no per-client coach override or approved calculation using body/activity inputs. Optional macro targets, hydration/habit goals and richer meal scheduling are absent. Automatic adjustment supports two triggers and a bounded calorie change.
2. **Case teaching is static.** Eight category forms gather cases. Coverage checks for a confirmed case in each category; compilation can return textual gaps, but there is no adaptive scenario/coverage engine or structured semantic contradiction resolution. Held-out evaluation checks action, target and case reference, rather than complete recipe/portion/grocery recommendations and rationale.
3. **Coach intervention cannot yet edit and deliver an individual corrected week.** There is no dedicated assign/amend/archive workflow with effective dates and reasons. Resolving an exception does not itself produce the corrected plan. Sampled audits and outcome/exception analytics are missing.
4. **Old recipe and food versions remain candidates.** Immutable records can reference a superseded version, but catalog selection includes every version. Updates do not retire obsolete facts from fresh generation.
5. **Recorded intake is not yet a full nutrition tracker.** Planned-day nutrient totals exist. Consumed-day totals, macro/weight trends and plan-versus-recorded comparisons do not. The Twin contains meal/day counts and recent check-ins. Weekly generation does not use the diary/Twin as model context.
6. **Capture corrections need more work.** Photo amount edits do not automatically rescale estimated nutrients, and recognized foods are not grounded through a separate food-fact lookup. Later diary corrections only edit name/date/kcal/notes. Separate photo-consent withdrawal exists in the API but has no matching client control. Unsupported barcode camera browsers fall back to manual digits.
7. **Automatic week recovery is incomplete.** Failed/blocked generation intents and stable scheduled keys have no retry/reconciliation workflow. Restoring a provider alone will not reliably resume the same due week. A fresh manual request is a workaround.
8. **Groceries represent ingredient quantities.** Purchase-pack conversions, raw-to-purchased amounts and explicit leftovers/preparation inventory remain to build.

The relevant implementation is in `apps/api/src/nutrition.ts`, `nutrition-schedule.ts`, `meal-capture.ts`, `packages/domain/src/nutrition.ts`, `packages/providers/src/food.ts` and the nutrition/capture UI. Tests are in `tests/nutrition.test.ts` and `tests/meal-capture.test.ts`.

## Finance and operational limits

The immutable ledger, refund/dispute accounting, cost records, holds and payout reservations are substantial implemented components. They are not an autonomous finance department. Destination review, settlement evidence, usage statements, close, payout preparation/execution and confirmed bank finality require operator actions. The worker handles email and nutrition, not monthly finance reconciliation. A successful Lean response is not treated as proof of payment.

The daily AI request cap is not a currency spending limit. Complete storage, infrastructure, messaging and voice cost allocation and margin monitoring are absent. Commission/fee allocation and financial policies still require operator decisions; this audit makes no legal or account-capability conclusion.

Local subscriber erasure exists with retained finance/audit evidence. Provider and backup deletion are recorded review/deadline requirements, not automated deletion. Trainer/workspace closure and ownership transfer need implementation. Actual approved legal documents also need a publishing mechanism, not just content supplied by an operator.

The deployment handoff has code and test evidence. No DigitalOcean environment, live HTTPS, automatic release, restore exercise, incident alert or production load result was demonstrated. Deployment remains assigned to Claude. No deployment action or cloud browser was used for this audit.

## Recommended completion order

1. **Repair existing promises and transitions:** safety holds and escalation delivery; billing servicing/idempotency; team invitations; preference readback/delivery; legal version wiring; blocked nutrition recovery and current food/recipe selection.
2. **Complete the coach's core product:** individual nutrition targets and a client-plan editor; stronger case teaching/evaluation; qualified automatic workout runtime; richer scheduled programs, progress and diary totals. Keep automatic behavior within evaluated capabilities.
3. **Finish the operator and trainer workflows:** real specialist admin pages, finance statements/reconciliation, complete billing history/refund selection, booking payments, lifecycle messages and branded asset/installation controls.
4. **Implement the remaining contracted integrations:** WHOOP/Zepp, domains and the optional premium voice/native paths according to launch priority. Keep unavailable capabilities accurately labeled until their code and qualification exist.
5. **Qualify the enabled release:** real coach/model/food checks, account-specific Stripe/Lean and email journeys, reviewed content/policies, device/accessibility/security/load checks, and demonstrated deployment/restore/rollback through the separate deployment handoff.

These are ordered work areas, not a completion date or permission request. Bespoke model training, per-trainer native App Store applications, a social network/marketplace, computer-vision form correction and a gym ERP remain outside the agreed initial scope; they are not counted as missing launch work.
