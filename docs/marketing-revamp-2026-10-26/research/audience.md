# Buyer research for the trainsyou marketing revamp (26 Oct 2026)

**How much weight each source carries:**
- **[Official]**: a government or legal text, or a regulator announcement.
- **[A]**: an independent study with a large sample.
- **[B]**: a large dataset from a vendor or operator, with its method disclosed.
- **[C]**: a vendor's marketing or a price guide published by someone who sells the service. The direction is useful; the size of the number is suspect.
- **[D]**: something a company told the press, a small sample, or an anecdote.
- **[E]**: expert opinion or theory.

Customer phrases quoted under the personas are hypotheses for interviews to test. Nobody actually said them.

## 1. Main conclusions

1. **Sell the AI training, and show it under the trainer's control.** Trainers like using AI themselves but fear unsafe output: in a coach survey of undisclosed size, 54% named inaccurate or unsafe advice as their top concern and 77% said AI can never replace a human coach [C]. Consumers prefer humans: 10% prefer AI workout guidance and 52% lean human-first [B]. The hero should therefore show the AI drafting and the trainer approving, as a real product screen. Use "your method, coached by AI, with you behind it", not "an AI replaces sessions".
2. **The follower is paying for the trainer, not for AI.** AI-only coaching is moving towards free: Future's new AI app is free while its human-coached tier is $199 a month [D]. The follower's reason to pay is the trainer's method and name, and the fact that the trainer handles anything serious. This matches the supportive-accountability model, where human support improves adherence through accountability to a coach the person sees as trustworthy and expert [E].
3. **In the UAE, trust in AI depends on conditions.** 65% would rely on information from AI, and 84% would trust it more if assured of trustworthy use [A]. The safety floor, approvals and rollback should be a full selling section, not a footnote. The skill's pitch-page rule agrees: for cautious buyers, what the system refuses to do persuades.
4. **Price is the follower's barrier.** 58% of people who don't go to a gym say price is the main reason, and budget gyms start at AED 99 a month [B]. One-to-one sessions cost AED 70–700+ [C]. The follower calculator and coach pages should present a monthly price for the trainer's method between those two anchors.
5. **Credentials are now regulated.** Dubai Sports Council's Mu'Ahal programme makes credential verification mandatory, starting with gym and personal trainers (announced 13 July 2026) [Official]. Trainers will respond to "your credentials, your name". The platform must not claim it verifies credentials.
6. **No testimonials exist, and none may be invented.** Proof has to come from three things:
   - showing the product: the approval queue, the scripted `/demo`, and the blind "Compare my replies" test;
   - open arithmetic: commission bands and calculators with sources;
   - stated constraints: the safety floor, what is labelled, data export and deletion.

## 3. Personas

### T1. The capped gym trainer (primary)
- **Who:** usually an expatriate aged 25–38, employed at a Dubai or Abu Dhabi gym or freelancing on a gym floor. 2k–30k Instagram followers. Sells sessions at AED 150–350, with the gym taking 30–60%.
- **Motivation:** income that is not tied to hours; something of their own.
- **Triggers:**
  - fully booked but income has plateaued;
  - followers DM "do you do online?" but can't afford sessions;
  - clients leaving the UAE;
  - injury or burnout;
  - the Ramadan or summer slump;
  - the new Mu'Ahal credential checks making their professional status visible.
- **Objections:**
  - "It will replace me or make me look cheap";
  - no time to set up;
  - "Will my followers pay?";
  - gym contract or licence worries.
- **Their words:** "I'm fully booked and still not earning more." "My DMs are full of people who can't afford me." "I can't clone myself." "I don't want a generic AI plan with my name on it."

### T2. The online coach already selling (primary, highest value)
- **Who:** 10k–200k followers, selling plans or WhatsApp check-ins at AED 400–1,200 a month. May already use an app such as Trainerize, and uses ChatGPT privately.
- **Motivation:** scale without losing their voice; take back their evenings.
- **Triggers:** check-in load, clients churning, WhatsApp chaos, losing clients to cheap AI apps.
- **Objections:**
  - "What's your cut?";
  - the cost of switching;
  - "My clients must feel it's me";
  - "The AI will say something I'd never say";
  - who owns the client relationship and data.
- **Their words:** "Check-ins are killing me." "I answer WhatsApp till midnight." "It has to sound like me." "What happens to my clients if I leave?"

### T3. The specialist coach: women, pre- and postnatal, or Arabic-speaking (primary for trust)
- **Who:** often a female coach serving women who want privacy or a female coach. Arabic-speaking or mixed audience. Safety-critical niches.
- **Motivation:** reach women who won't train in a mixed gym, at a price they accept.
- **Triggers:** women-only demand (46% call it essential [B]); the perception that women pay more; Ramadan.
- **Objections:**
  - AI safety with pregnancy or medical conditions;
  - cultural fit;
  - quality of the Arabic;
  - privacy of photos and health data;
  - "I must approve what goes out".
- **Their words:** "My clients won't go to a mixed gym." "One wrong answer to a pregnant client and I'm finished." "Does it work in Arabic?"

### T4. The small studio owner (secondary)
- **Who:** a studio with 3–8 coaches.
- **Motivation:** turn the house method into a product and keep consistency across coaches.
- **Objections:** what staff can see and do, attribution, who owns the brand. The product has answers: team roles, finance hidden from staff, owner owns brand and Brain (`marketing-content.ts:1815-1825`).

### Followers (who pay through the trainer's site)
- **F1, priced out of one-to-one:** follows the trainer and can't pay AED 250 a session. Wants a plan, accountability and the trainer's "real" method. Objections: "Is it really them or a bot?", "Will it handle my knee?", "Can I cancel?", "Does it work when I'm fasting?".
- **F2, privacy first:** a woman who wants a female coach, home workouts, Arabic and no gym intimidation (50% of women are affected [B]).

## 4. Objection, answer and proof, tied to real capabilities

1. **"It will replace me or dilute my brand."**
   - **Answer:** it coaches your method under your name; you are always the person it escalates to; you can take over any client.
   - **Proof:** go-live in "Waits for me" mode, where every reply waits for you (`docs/features/setup-wizard.md:86-92`, `docs/features/brain-teach.md:46`). The FAQ (`packages/contracts/src/marketing-content.ts:653-656`). Digital replies are labelled (`:1567-1568`). Show the one-queue Today screen (`docs/features/trainer-workspace.md`).
2. **"Unsafe advice under my name."**
   - **Answer:** safety rules in code, outside the AI. Pain pauses the workout and comes to you. Each release is checked on your held-out cases before it goes live, and you can roll back.
   - **Proof:** `docs/features/safety-floor.md` (the code safety floor held all 13 red-flag messages in the internal trial set, which is a fixture result, not a live accuracy figure). `docs/features/brain-check.md`. FAQs at `marketing-content.ts:650-651`, `:1734-1741` and `:2187-2188`.
   - **Do not claim** any accuracy percentage: real-world trainer fidelity is unmeasured (`docs/PERSONAL_COACHING_2026-10-13.md`).
3. **"I need control over what it says."**
   - **Answer:**
     - rules are written in plain language and you approve each one;
     - flagged rules are never included in "Approve all";
     - your corrections become suggested rules you confirm;
     - you choose which routine changes run automatically;
     - you can rate the Brain blind against your own replies.
   - **Proof:** `docs/features/brain-teach.md`, `docs/features/brain-check.md`, `docs/features/brain-fidelity.md` (Compare my replies, rated 1–5 on five dimensions), `marketing-content.ts:1023-1024`.
4. **"Who owns my method and data?"**
   - **Answer:** it is private to your workspace, isolated at the database layer, never used for another trainer, and not fine-tuned on you. You can export or delete. Your voice is used only with your consent, which you can withdraw.
   - **Proof:** `marketing-content.ts:669-672`, `:957-958`, `:2150-2151`, `:2310-2311` and `:1420-1430`; `docs/features/trainer-voice.md`.
5. **"Whose brand do followers see?"**
   - **Answer:** yours: your site, an app followers install from the browser with your name and icon, a free subdomain, and an optional own domain. The website draft is built from your onboarding answers, your own photos and your real prices.
   - **Proof:** `docs/ONBOARDING_WEBSITE_2026-10-16.md`; `marketing-content.ts:2318-2319`, `:1081-1082` and `:2492-2493`.
6. **"Will my followers pay?"**
   - **Answer:** some will. Here is an estimate for your own numbers, with stated assumptions; you set the AED price.
   - **Proof:** `packages/domain/src/marketing-calculators.ts`; `marketing-content.ts:2191-2192` and `:2053-2058`.
7. **"My followers can't afford much."**
   - **Answer:** a monthly price for your method sits between AED 99 budget gyms and AED 200–350 sessions. Nutrition is an optional tier; voice is an add-on.
   - **Proof:** the worked example of AED 199 a month (`marketing-content.ts:1974`) and `:1357-1358`.
8. **"I have no time to set up."**
   - **Answer:** a guided wizard of about 15 minutes in six steps, by chat or short form, that saves as you go. Your website is drafted for you.
   - **Proof:** `docs/features/setup-wizard.md:4` and `:31`.
   - **Caveat:** going live needs an approved rule, a practice quiz round and three of your own cases (`brain-teach.md:46`). Say "start in about 15 minutes", not "launch in 15 minutes", and keep it consistent with the FAQ at `marketing-content.ts:846-847`.
9. **"What's your cut?"**
   - **Answer:** free to join. Commission only when subscribers pay, in marginal bands of 25%, 20%, 15% and 10%, plus payment processing. Paid out monthly to a UAE bank account in AED.
   - **Proof:** `marketing-content.ts:657-668`, `:1908-1909`, `:1981-1982` and `:2200-2201`.
   - Never mention AI costs (owner decision, 30 Sep).
10. **"What about my licence and the law?"**
    - **Answer:** you can set up and teach before your trade licence is ready; payments and launch have their own checks. Promoting your own services is exempt from the advertiser permit (information only).
    - **Proof:** `marketing-content.ts:2488-2489`, `:2846-2847` and `:2218-2219`.
    - **Do not** imply the platform verifies credentials or satisfies Mu'Ahal.
11. **"Does it work in Arabic?"**
    - **Answer, honestly:** the follower experience is English and Arabic, including right-to-left layouts. The trainer area is English-only, and content the coach writes is not auto-translated.
    - **Proof:** `marketing-content.ts:1222-1223`; `docs/features/setup-wizard.md` ("Not done here").
    - This is a real gap for T3.
12. **"Health data and privacy."**
    - **Answer:** consent for each use, export and delete, workspace isolation, passkeys and authenticator apps, and audited operator access that requires a second sign-in.
    - **Proof:** `marketing-content.ts:2086` and `:2154-2159`.
    - **Do not** claim "PDPL certified" or "PDPL compliant": the regulations are pending and health data is carved out of that law.
13. **"How will my followers pay?"**
    - **Answer:** by card in AED through Stripe.
    - **Apple Pay is not verified.** Subscription checkout does not restrict payment methods, but bookings and module passes are card-only (`apps/api/src/finance-checkout.ts:703`, `finance-bookings.ts:166`, `module-passes.ts:225`). In Stripe Checkout, wallets appear only when enabled in the account and the domain is verified. Do not advertise Apple Pay until it has been checked live.
14. **"What about Ramadan or fasting clients?"**
    - **Answer, honestly:** fasting meal timing goes to the coach, and religious fasting is not mistaken for disordered eating.
    - **Proof:** `packages/domain/src/nutrition.ts:412-415`, `packages/domain/src/red-flags.ts:738-746`.
    - There is no Ramadan programming feature, so don't claim one.

## 5. Implications for messaging and page structure

**Messaging**
- **Lead with what the trainer gets and show the product:** "Your method, coaching every follower: you approve, it learns." The hero should be the approval queue with a Brain draft, not a problem statement. Keep "beyond your hours" as the economic line underneath.
- **Use their words:** method, check-ins, DMs, your name, approve, sounds like me, AED, monthly. **Avoid:** "replace", "passive income", "clone", "AI trainer" without the trainer present, vendor names, accuracy claims. Keep "frontier model" conditional on qualification, as the existing site does (`docs/features/marketing-site.md:34-36`).
- **Follower-facing copy** (coach pages and the follower calculator) should say "[Trainer]'s method, every day, with [Trainer] behind it". The AI is how it is delivered, not what is being sold.
- **Calculator tension:** the skill advises understating, while the owner chose to headline the strong case with labels. Keep the owner's decision, and keep cautious and typical one tap away.

**Page structure for all three concepts**
- **Home:** product hero; a "you stay in control" strip (Waits for me, safety floor, rollback); how teaching works in three steps; what followers get; a mini earnings or follower calculator; commission bands; a constraints section; a single CTA, "Start coaching (free)".
- **Trainer Brain, How it works and Demo:** make the blind "Compare my replies" test and the scripted decisions the main proof.
- **Pricing:** show the commission arithmetic in full plus a session-equivalent comparison. Nothing to pay upfront.
- **Security:** consent, export and delete, isolation, voice consent. Make no certification claims.
- **Specialty and UAE pages:** a women's or pre- and postnatal angle, women-only demand data with sources, honest Arabic scope, Ramadan routing, and Mu'Ahal context marked "information only".
- **FAQ:** order it by the objections above.
- **Kamran voice assistant:** use it as a live answer to objections.
- **CTA:** first person ("Start my coaching site"); free to join. Trainers who start before having a licence can be routed to early access.

**Three concept angles that fit this research**
- **A, Control:** trust-first, built around the approval queue and the safety floor; aimed at T3 and T1.
- **B, Beyond your hours:** economics-first, built around the calculators and commission arithmetic; aimed at T1 and T2.
- **C, Your method, alive:** demo-first, built around teaching, blind comparison and the trainer's branded follower app; aimed at T2.
