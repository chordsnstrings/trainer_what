### Review round 1 (same branch)

Current state; it supersedes the original build's text below where they differ (notably: automatic estimation at month close is now **off** by default, and the domain figures in metrics are per currency). Twenty-two review findings were checked against the code; all majors and the cheap minors were fixed with regression tests; the rest are declined below with reasons.

- **Month-close re-run (major).** When the month already has a usage statement, `executeFinanceJob` reuses it (rate and charge) and goes on to close and the payout; it no longer recalculates, so an invoice correction or a reviewed rate recorded after the charge can no longer make every re-run fail with `USAGE_INTENT_CONFLICT`. A reviewed rate other than the approved one is recorded in the result (`rateOverride`) and the event `finance.automation_rate_override`.
- **Estimation off by default; unsent calls cost nothing (major).** `FINANCE_ESTIMATE_UNRESOLVED_USAGE` defaults to off (today's behaviour: unresolved calls block automatic close until reconciled or estimated by an operator). Voice speech, guided audio and transcription that fail before the provider request (row still `reserved`) are released at zero (`costNotSent`) instead of `unknown`; sent calls without an answer stay `unknown` for the invoice. Estimation computes the 90-day average only for rows without a stored estimate and updates in one set-based statement.
- **Provider pricing (majors).** Refused while any call of that provider and month has no estimate (`ROWS_WITHOUT_ESTIMATE`; the total covers it, so it would be counted twice); refused when a resumed run under a reference sees a different set of calls (`REFERENCE_SCOPE_CHANGED`); a total outside 0.5-2 times the estimates needs `acknowledgeFactor` (`FACTOR_REVIEW_REQUIRED`); shares are rounded cumulatively so they add up exactly; rows reconciled one by one now count as already priced (a NULL in the adjustable test left them out of the fixed total, found by the new test); provider names are lower-cased; month filters use a created_at range (index-covered), also in `periodUsage`. Audit rows before and after the run, a tenant event per changed workspace, and the charged workspaces' charged and current figures in the response.
- **Charged months (minor).** `reconcileModelUsage` on a charged month returns `correctionAfterCharge` and records `finance.usage_corrected_after_charge`; Finance operations lists each statement with its rate and, when different, the month's current priced charge and the difference (not charged); the usage-charge preview shows the posted charge (USD, rate, AED) beside the current figures and the rate the charge would use (`chargeRate`: reviewed, the automation's approved rate, or the default).
- **Rate revisions (major).** `POST /exchange-rates` refuses a revision when usage statements for that month were posted at another rate (`POSTED_STATEMENTS_DIFFER`) unless `acknowledgePostedStatements`; the audit row (same transaction) records the previous rate, revision and source and the count; an unchanged rate is audited as `finance.exchange_rate_unchanged`; `/admin/metrics` flags months with charges at another rate (`usageChargedAtOtherRate`). The default-rate help text says metrics and previews use it while automatic close keeps its approved rate.

The refund reversal selects the original journal's currency and passes `{ currency }` to `journal()` (ignored on this branch, where every journal is AED; honoured once `core/domain-pricing`'s `journal()` option merges). After a refund's failure, no later pending, succeeded or `charge.refunded` snapshot, and no order-failure refund, posts it.
